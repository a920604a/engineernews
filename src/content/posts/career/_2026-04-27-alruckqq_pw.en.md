## TL;DR
Can "going to work" really ruin someone? The answer is yes. Prolonged work and high-pressure work environments can lead to severe physical and mental health problems for employees.

## What's Going On and Why It's a Problem Now
We often think of going to work as a normal part of life, but in reality, work stress is one of the main causes of employee physical and mental health issues. According to the World Health Organization, work-related stress can lead to anxiety, depression, and even suicidal thoughts. So, why does going to work lead to these problems? The reason is that employees in a high-pressure work environment may face long working hours, lack of rest time, and unreasonable demands.

## How It Works
In a high-pressure work environment, employees may face the following challenges:

This flowchart shows how a high-pressure work environment can lead to physical and mental health problems, affecting work performance and employee satisfaction. When employees face a high-pressure work environment, they may feel anxious and depressed, affecting their work performance and satisfaction.

## The Difference from a Good Work Environment
A good work environment is one where employees can work comfortably and safely, with their needs and rights respected. The main difference between a high-pressure work environment and a good work environment is the working conditions and treatment of employees. In a good work environment, employees can enjoy reasonable working hours, rest time, and reasonable demands. Employees working in a good work environment may feel more comfortable and safe, leading to improved work performance and satisfaction.

## Putting It into Practice
In reality, employees can take the following measures to protect themselves:

* Set realistic work goals and priorities
* Maintain good time management
* Take regular breaks and relax
* Seek support and help

However, these measures may be limited by the work environment. For example, in a high-pressure work environment, employees may not have enough time to take breaks and relax. Therefore, finding a good work environment is also crucial.
