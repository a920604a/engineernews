**Is Working Holiday Abroad a New Opportunity or Just a Detour?**

## TL;DR
Working holiday abroad can provide new perspectives and opportunities, but it may also be just a detour back to square one.

## What is it?
Working holiday abroad is a way to combine work and travel, allowing you to work in a foreign country for a period of time while enjoying the local culture and lifestyle. This approach can broaden your horizons, teach you new skills, and facilitate interactions with people from different cultures.

## Why is it important?
Working holiday abroad can offer new opportunities and challenges, prompting you to reassess your life and future direction. It can also help you establish new connections and networks, expanding your professional possibilities.

## How it works
The typical process for working holiday abroad is as follows:

```mermaid
graph LR
    A[Decide on working holiday abroad] -->|Plan and prepare|> B[Apply for visa and work permit]
    B -->|Find job opportunities|> C[Start working and living abroad]
    C -->|Learn and grow|> D[Reassess and plan for the future]
```

## Difference from working domestically
Working domestically may provide comfort and familiarity, but it may also limit your perspectives and opportunities. Working holiday abroad, on the other hand, can offer new challenges and opportunities for growth and learning.

## Summary
Working holiday abroad is suitable for those who want to broaden their horizons, learn new skills, and interact with people from different cultures. It is also suitable for those who want to reassess their life and future direction. However, working holiday abroad may also be just a detour back to square one if you lack clear goals and planning.
