## TL;DR
This article will introduce a 5-step approach to alleviate panic symptoms.

## Prerequisites
No specific environment or tools are required, but some knowledge of mental health and self-awareness are necessary.

## Steps
### Step 1: Acknowledge Panic
Acknowledging your panic feelings is the first crucial step. Don't try to suppress or deny your emotions; instead, face them head-on. Tell yourself, "I'm feeling panicked right now, and that's okay."

### Step 2: Deep Breathing
Deep breathing can help calm you down and reduce anxiety. Try the following:
- Close your eyes
- Slowly inhale for 4 seconds
- Slowly exhale for 4 seconds
- Repeat the process 3-5 times

### Step 3: Observe Physical Sensations
During a panic episode, your body may experience discomfort, such as a racing heart or sweaty palms. Observe your physical sensations and tell yourself, "What is my body experiencing right now?"

### Step 4: Distract Yourself
Distracting yourself can help you temporarily forget about the panic. Try doing something unrelated to the panic, such as:
- Listening to music
- Looking at pictures
- Doing simple actions

### Step 5: Self-Comfort
Finally, try to comfort yourself with reassuring words. Tell yourself, "I can handle this panic; I've overcome many challenges before, and I can do it again."

## Complete Example
No specific code is required, but here's a simple self-comforting exercise:

```
I'm feeling panicked right now, but I can handle it.
I'll take deep breaths, observe my physical sensations, and distract myself.
I'll tell myself I can get through this again.
```
