**Introduction**
Recently, Luo Xiang mentioned a phrase in a video that resonated deeply with me: "The older we get, the more likely we are to be 'trapped' by life." This phrase seems to have struck a chord with many people, including myself. So, what exactly is it about growing older that makes us feel more pressured and constrained by life? This article will delve into this question and attempt to provide an answer.

## TL;DR
The older we get, the more likely we are to be 'trapped' by life because we become increasingly bound by established frameworks.

## What is it?
Being 'trapped' by life refers to the feeling of being constrained by established frameworks and responsibilities, losing our freedom and creativity. This feeling may arise from the gap between our expectations and the reality of our lives.

## Why is it important
This issue is important because it affects our life satisfaction and mental health. When we feel 'trapped' by life, we may feel frustrated, anxious, and desperate, which can even impact our physical health.

## How it works
Below is the process of framework constraints mentioned by Luo Xiang:

```mermaid
graph LR
    A[Our expectations] -->|Gap with reality|> B[Our actual life]
    B -->|Framework constraints|> C[Our freedom and creativity]
    C -->|Limited|> D[Our life satisfaction]
```

## Difference from "freedom" and "creativity"
Unlike "freedom" and "creativity", being 'trapped' by life is a more subtle and insidious phenomenon. Freedom and creativity refer to what we can do and create, while being 'trapped' by life refers to the feeling of being constrained by established frameworks, losing our freedom and creativity.

## Conclusion
If you feel 'trapped' by life, remember that you are not alone. Luo Xiang's words remind us that life's frameworks and responsibilities are unavoidable, but we can try to break through and change them. As long as we are willing to explore and try new possibilities, we can regain our freedom and creativity.
