Why Overthinking the Future Leads to Nihilism?

Introduction:
This article explores why people who constantly think too far into the future tend to fall into nihilism, and how adjusting one's time scale can help avoid this phenomenon. By the end of this article, readers will understand how to avoid the negative emotions caused by overthinking the future.

# TL;DR
Adjust your time scale to avoid the nihilism caused by overthinking the future.

# What is Nihilism
Nihilism is a philosophical idea that life has no meaning or purpose. Thinking too far into the future can lead people to feel the impermanence and brevity of life, resulting in feelings of nihilism and despair.

# Why it Matters
Overthinking the future can lead to stress and anxiety, affecting one's mental and physical health. Moreover, excessive focus on the future can cause people to neglect the happiness and well-being of the present moment.

# How it Works
When people think too far into the future, they may experience a feeling of "time dilation," where time seems to slow down, accompanied by feelings of stress and anxiety. This sensation can lead to feelings of nihilism and despair.

# The Difference from Planning
Planning refers to specific arrangements and goal-setting for the future, which differs from overthinking the future. Planning can help people organize their time and resources more effectively, achieving their goals.

# Conclusion
Adjusting one's time scale can help people avoid the nihilism caused by overthinking the future. By focusing on the present and setting realistic goals, people can better manage their time and emotions, leading to improved mental and physical health.
