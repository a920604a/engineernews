**Analyzing "EP674 At 29, I Spent Three Months Overthinking Over a Jacket"：The Dangers of Overthinking**

## TL;DR
Overthinking can lead to decision paralysis, wasting a significant amount of time and energy.

## What is it
Overthinking refers to spending too much time thinking about something, especially when making a decision, repeatedly thinking and considering different options, which can ultimately lead to decision paralysis.

## Why is it important
Overthinking can result in low decision-making efficiency, wasting time and energy, and even affecting daily life and work. It can also lead to anxiety and stress, impacting mental health.

## How it works
When faced with a decision, overthinking can lead to an endless cycle of thinking, repeatedly considering different options and outcomes. This process can be illustrated as follows:

```mermaid
graph LR
    A[Decision Required] -->|Needs Thinking|> B[Thinking]
    B -->|Thinking Result|> C[Still Undecided]
    C -->|Re-thinking|> B
    B -->|Final Decision|> D[Decision Made]
```

## Difference from Procrastination
Overthinking and procrastination are both manifestations of low decision-making efficiency, but they have different underlying causes. Procrastination is the intentional avoidance of doing something, whereas overthinking is spending too much time thinking about something.

## In Conclusion
Overthinking is a common problem that can lead to low decision-making efficiency. If you find yourself stuck in an overthinking cycle, try setting time limits to increase decision-making efficiency and avoid wasting time and energy.
