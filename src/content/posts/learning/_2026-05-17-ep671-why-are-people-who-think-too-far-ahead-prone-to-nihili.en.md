**Exploring the Connection between Long-Term Thinking and Nihilism**

## TL;DR
Excessive long-term thinking can lead to nihilism, as overemphasizing the future can make individuals feel that the present is meaningless.

## What is it?
Long-term thinking refers to an individual's ability to think about and plan for their future goals and objectives. This type of thinking can help people set directions, make decisions, and achieve goals. However, when taken to an extreme, it can lead to an overemphasis on the future, causing individuals to neglect the value and meaning of the present.

## Why is it important?
Long-term thinking is crucial for personal growth and development, as it helps individuals set goals and directions, achieving long-term success. However, when overdone, it can lead to feelings of meaninglessness and emptiness in the present, potentially resulting in nihilism.

## How it works
The process of long-term thinking can be described using the following flowchart:

```mermaid
graph LR
    A[Set goals] -->|Set long-term goals|> B[Plan route]
    B -->|Assess risks and challenges|> C[Adjust route]
    C -->|Execute plan|> D[Assess progress]
    D -->|Adjust route|> C
```

The process of long-term thinking requires individuals to continuously set goals, plan routes, assess risks and challenges, execute plans, and assess progress. However, when individuals overemphasize the future, they may neglect the value and meaning of the present, leading to nihilism.

## Difference from Existentialism
Existentialism focuses on the individual's existence and experience in the present, rather than overemphasizing future goals and plans. Existentialism can help individuals better experience the present, finding meaning and value in life.

|  | Long-term thinking | Existentialism |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Focus | Future goals and plans | Present existence and experience |
| Emphasis | Setting goals and direction | Experiencing the present and finding meaning |

## Conclusion
Long-term thinking is essential, but excessive focus on the future can lead to nihilism. Individuals need to find a balance between setting goals and directions while experiencing the present and finding meaning. Existentialism offers a different perspective, helping individuals better experience the present.
