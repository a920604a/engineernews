## TL;DR
Slashing isn't just about making money; it's a way to realize personal growth and find self-worth and breathing room.

## What is it and why now?
Slashing is a new work model where an individual has one or more side hustles or hobbies outside of their primary job, typically to realize personal value or increase income. However, slashing isn't just about making money; it's a way to achieve self-realization and growth. In today's society, work pressure and living costs are increasing, and people are seeking new ways to realize their value and increase income. With the prevalence of the internet and social platforms, people can easily find their interests and passions and share them with others, achieving personal growth and value.

## How it works
The process of developing a slash typically includes the following steps:
```mermaid
graph LR
    A[Discover interests] --> B[Learn skills]
    B --> C[Create works]
    C --> D[Share works]
    D --> E[Get feedback]
    E --> F[Improve works]
```
First, people need to discover their interests and passions, then learn relevant skills. Next, create works and share them with others, getting feedback and evaluation. Finally, improve works and continue learning, achieving personal growth and value.

## Differences from full-time work
The main difference between full-time work and slashing is the nature and goal of work. Full-time work is typically for economic income and social status, while slashing is for personal value and growth. Full-time work requires a lot of time and energy, while slashing can be done according to individual interests and time schedules.

## Differences from side jobs
The main difference between slashing and side jobs is motivation and goal. Side jobs are typically for increasing income or achieving economic goals, while slashing is for personal value and growth. Side jobs require a lot of time and energy, while slashing can be done according to individual interests and time schedules.

## How it works in practice
Slashing can bring people a sense of achievement and pride, making them feel valuable. However, slashing also requires time and energy, continuous learning, and improvement. Additionally, slashing requires facing market and competitive pressure, requiring constant innovation and improvement. To avoid slashing becoming another job, we need to find our interests and passions, set clear goals and boundaries, and maintain a balance between work and life.
