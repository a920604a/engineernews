# Is Being Loved Contingent on Making Contributions?

## TL;DR
The relationship between love and contribution.

## What Is It
The relationship between love and contribution is a complex issue that involves psychological, philosophical, and social aspects. In this article, we will explore this question and attempt to find the true relationship between love and contribution.

## Why Is It Important
This question is important because it involves people's self-worth and self-esteem. If we believe that only those who contribute are worthy of love, does that mean those who don't contribute are not worthy of love? This idea can lead to people feeling inferior and unaccepted.

## How It Works
The relationship between love and contribution can be described by the following flowchart:

```mermaid

    A[Love] -->|Influences|> B[Self-Worth]
    B -->|Influences|> C[Self-Esteem]

    D -->|Influences|> E[Societal Recognition]
```

In this flowchart, love can influence a person's self-worth, which in turn affects self-esteem, interpersonal relationships, and societal recognition. Contributions can be seen as a manifestation of love, expressing care and appreciation for others.

## Difference from "Self-Worth"
The relationship between love and contribution is different from the relationship between self-worth. Self-worth refers to a person's evaluation of their own value, while the relationship between love and contribution refers to how people view the value of others. Self-worth can be enhanced through contributions, but this does not mean that only those who contribute are worthy of love.

## Conclusion
The relationship between love and contribution is complex and cannot be simply reduced to "only those who contribute are worthy of love." Every person deserves to be loved and respected, regardless of whether they contribute or not. To establish healthy and equal interpersonal relationships, we must abandon the idea that love is conditional on contribution.
