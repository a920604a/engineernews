【Ten-Minute Psychology】Don't Want to Conform but Afraid of Hurting Feelings? Learn to Gently Set Your Boundaries

## TL;DR
Gently setting your own boundaries is an effective strategy for handling interpersonal relationships.

## What is it
Gently setting your own boundaries is a communication technique that involves expressing your limits and needs in a gentle manner when interacting with others, avoiding sacrificing your own interests by conforming to others.

## Why is it important
In real life, many people face this dilemma: to maintain good interpersonal relationships and avoid hurting others' feelings, but not wanting to conform to others' requests. Gently setting your own boundaries can help you find a balance in such situations, protecting your own interests while avoiding hurting others' feelings.

## How it works
The principle of gently setting your own boundaries can be illustrated with the following flowchart:
```mermaid
graph LR
    A[Receive request from others] --> B[Assess request]
    B -->|Meets own needs| C[Accept request]
    B -->|Does not meet own needs| D[Gently decline]
    D --> E[Explain reasons]
    E --> F[Find alternative solutions]
```
First, when you receive a request from others, you need to assess whether it meets your own needs. If it does, you can accept the request; if not, you need to gently decline and explain your reasons. Finally, find alternative solutions that satisfy both parties' needs.

## Difference from Conformity
The main difference between gently setting your own boundaries and conformity lies in their core objectives. Conformity aims to avoid conflict and hurt others' feelings, while gently setting your own boundaries aims to protect your own interests and needs. The former may sacrifice your own interests, while the latter can find a balance, protecting your own interests while avoiding hurting others' feelings.

## Summary
Gently setting your own boundaries is an effective communication technique suitable for those who don't want to conform but are afraid of hurting others. By gently setting your own boundaries, you can protect your own interests, avoid sacrificing your needs, and avoid hurting others' feelings.
