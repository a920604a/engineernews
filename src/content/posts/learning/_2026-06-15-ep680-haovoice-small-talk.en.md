**Finding True Connections in Books**

## TL;DR
The true purpose of reading is to find connections between the book and oneself.

## What is it?
A "connection" refers to the process of resonating with the book's content, linking it to one's own life experiences, or gaining new knowledge. This connection can be an emotional resonance, a spark of inspiration, or even a life-changing experience.

## Why is it important?
In our busy lives, we often buy many books but fail to finish reading them. This can lead to feelings of guilt and failure. However, Haovoice believes that the focus should be on finding connections with the book, rather than finishing it. These connections can bring true value and gain to the reader.

## How does it work?
When we read a book, our minds generate thoughts and feelings. Some thoughts may be directly related to the content, while others may be indirect associations. These thoughts and feelings are our connections to the book. The following flowchart illustrates this process:

```mermaid
graph LR
    A[Reading a book] -->|Generating thoughts and feelings|> B[Thoughts and feelings]
    B -->|Directly related to the content|> C[Directly related thoughts]
    B -->|Not directly related to the content|> D[Indirectly related thoughts]
    C -->|Inspiration and resonance|> E[Connection established]
    D -->|Inspiration and resonance|> E
```

## The difference from "finishing a book"
Instead of focusing on finishing a book, focus on finding connections with it. The former may lead to pressure and boredom, while the latter can bring true value and gain to the reader.

## Conclusion
Finding connections between the book and oneself is the true purpose of reading. Whether you're reading fiction, essays, or professional books, what matters is whether you can gain inspiration and resonance from the book. So, the next time you see a book in a store, don't hesitate – buy it and try to find connections with it.
