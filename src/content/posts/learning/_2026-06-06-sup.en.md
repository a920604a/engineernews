# Getting Started with Sup: A Simple and Efficient Video Conferencing Tool

## TL;DR
Sup is a simple and easy-to-use video conferencing tool that provides high-quality video calling experiences.

## What is Sup
Sup is a WebRTC-based video conferencing tool that allows users to make point-to-point or multi-party video calls. It offers a simple and intuitive interface that enables users to quickly start video meetings.

## Why is it important
Sup solves some of the problems associated with traditional video conferencing tools, such as complex setup processes and low-quality video calling experiences. Sup provides a simple and easy-to-use video conferencing solution, making it easier for users to communicate remotely.

## How it works
Here's how Sup works:

```mermaid
graph LR
    A[User] -->|Clicks link|> B[Sup Server]
    B -->|Establishes connection|> C[WebRTC Server]
    C -->|Establishes call|> D[Other users]
    D -->|Transmits video|> C
    C -->|Transmits video|> B
    B -->|Transmits video|> A
```

## Differences from other video conferencing tools
Compared to other video conferencing tools, Sup's advantages lie in its simple and intuitive interface and high-quality video calling experiences. Sup does not require users to go through complex setup processes, allowing them to quickly start video meetings.

## In summary
Sup is suitable for users who need to conduct simple and easy-to-use video conferences, especially teams or individuals who require remote communication. Sup's high-quality video calling experiences and simple interface make it an ideal video conferencing tool.
