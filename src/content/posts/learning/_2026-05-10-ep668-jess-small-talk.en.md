**Rethinking Property Management: From Vacant Houses to Commoditized Management**

In the real estate market, property management is a common concept, but its true meaning and value are often underestimated. This article delves into the concept, importance, operating principles, and differences between property management and related concepts.

## TL;DR
Property management is a systematic management profession that re-commoditizes vacant houses, providing more efficient rental services.

## What is Property Management?
Property management refers to professional companies or individuals providing rental services to property owners, including finding tenants, managing leases, and maintaining properties. This is a business outsourcing approach that allows property owners to enjoy more efficient rental services.

## Why is it Important?
Property management solves many problems for property owners, such as difficulty finding tenants, lease management hassles, and property maintenance responsibilities. By outsourcing these tasks to professional companies, property owners can save time and energy while increasing their income.

## How it Works
The operating process of property management is as follows:

```mermaid
graph LR
    A[Property Owner] -->|Delegate|> B[Property Management Company]
    B -->|Find Tenant|> C[Tenant]
    B -->|Manage Lease|> D[Lease]
    B -->|Maintain Property|> E[Property]
    C -->|Sign Contract|> D
    D -->|Pay Rent|> A
```

## Differences from Property Administration
Property management and property administration are related concepts in real estate management, but they have different focuses and application scenarios. Property administration primarily concerns daily property maintenance and management, while property management focuses on rental business. Property management emphasizes finding tenants, managing leases, and other business outsourcing services.

## Summary
Property management is a systematic management profession suitable for property owners who want to re-commoditize vacant houses and improve rental efficiency. Through property management, property owners can enjoy more efficient rental services, increase income, and save time.
