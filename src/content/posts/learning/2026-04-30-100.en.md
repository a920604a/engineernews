What Happens When a "Standard Man" is Quietly Drained by Society?

## TL;DR
Understand the predicaments and pressures faced by standard men in society.

## What is a "Standard Man"
A "standard man" refers to a male who conforms to traditional masculine roles and behavioral norms expected by society. These norms include traits such as strength, resilience, and emotional restraint.

## Why is it Important
Standard men face numerous pressures and predicaments, including:

* Societal expectations: Men are expected to meet certain standards, such as being strong, successful, and able to provide for their families.
* Difficulty expressing emotions: Men are taught to be strong and not show emotions, making it hard for them to express their feelings and needs.
* Physical and mental health issues: Standard men may neglect their physical and mental health, leading to various problems.

## How it Works
The dynamics of standard men in society can be illustrated by the following flowchart:

```mermaid

    A[Societal Expectations] -->|Pressure|> B[Masculine Role]
    B -->|Influence|> C[Emotional Expression]
```

## Differences from "Non-Standard Men"
Non-standard men are those who do not conform to traditional masculine roles and behavioral norms. Compared to standard men, non-standard men may face different pressures and predicaments, such as:

* Social recognition pressure: Non-standard men may face social disapproval and discrimination.
* Self-identity difficulties: Non-standard men may struggle with self-identity issues, such as not knowing who they are or how to express themselves.

## Conclusion
Standard men face many pressures and predicaments, and understanding these issues is crucial for improving men's physical and mental health. At the same time, society needs to be more inclusive and accepting of non-standard men, allowing everyone to express themselves freely.
