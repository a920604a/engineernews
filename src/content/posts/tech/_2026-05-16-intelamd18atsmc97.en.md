## TL;DR
Recently, the competition among semiconductor giants AMD, Intel, and TSMC has intensified, with the Trump administration's intervention, revealing the intense competition in the global semiconductor industry.

## What Happened
According to recent news reports, AMD's CEO, Su Zifeng, stated that they aim to poach TSMC's clients, including major customers like Apple and Tesla. On the other hand, the Trump administration is backing Intel, hoping to capture TSMC's market share. These events have sparked attention in the semiconductor industry.

## Why It Matters
These events are crucial for engineers and the industry, as they impact the competitive landscape and future development of the global semiconductor industry. The semiconductor industry is the foundation of modern technology, and the competition among major manufacturers will directly influence future technological advancements and product innovation.

## Technical Perspective
