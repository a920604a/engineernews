On June 8, 2026, Tim Cook took the stage at WWDC26. Everyone knew it would be his last time opening Apple's developer conference as CEO — on September 1, he transitions to Executive Chairman, handing the role to John Ternus. The keynote carried that weight, even if Apple didn't dramatize it.

## TL;DR

WWDC26's headline: Apple embedded Google Gemini into Siri, turning Apple Intelligence from a feature collection into a genuine cross-app AI layer. Cook exits in September, Ternus steps in, and Apple's AI strategy is clearly mid-repositioning.

## What Was Announced

### Siri's Rebuild

Siri is now powered by Google Gemini. The new version understands multi-turn conversation, pulls context across apps — ask it about your afternoon meeting with someone and it reads Mail, Calendar, and Messages to answer — and ships as its own standalone app while remaining integrated system-wide.

Craig Federighi's framing: "Data is only used to execute your request." Apple is betting its differentiation is privacy architecture, not model capability.

### Apple Intelligence Updates

This generation is more practical than the conceptual promises of a year ago:

- **Spatial Reframing**: Adjust a photo's composition and perspective after the fact
- **Extend**: AI-generated background expansion beyond a photo's original edges
- **Cross-app context**: Smart reply suggestions reference both Mail and Messages
- **Natural language Shortcuts**: Describe what you want, skip the manual setup

### iOS 27 and macOS Golden Gate

iOS 27 rolls back to iPhone 11 compatibility — unusually broad for Apple. Key AI additions: rebuilt Spotlight, AI-integrated Photos and Mail search, system-wide dictation with automatic punctuation.

macOS jumps from the Sequoia family to Golden Gate.

One side note: foldable iPhone references surfaced in iOS 27 beta. No announcement, but developers are already digging.

## Cook's Exit Read

Ternus didn't appear on stage at all — notable if Apple were actively positioning him as heir apparent. This could mean Cook got a clean farewell, or that Ternus's strategic direction isn't ready for public articulation yet.

The Google Gemini integration is arguably Cook's most consequential AI decision: embedding a longtime competitor's model at the core of Siri requires CEO-level conviction. How Ternus evolves that relationship — or doesn't — is the most interesting open question.

## Technical Take

**Gemini in Siri signals Apple's model strategy**: Apple is not trying to win on foundation model quality. Their bet is that privacy architecture + deep system integration is a more durable moat than building the best LLM.

**Cross-app context is technically hard**: Reading across Mail, Calendar, Messages requires fine-grained permission layers and carefully bounded data access. Apple's on-device + Private Cloud Compute split determines where the real capability ceiling is.

**The on-device scale problem**: Mobile hardware limits model size, but user expectations of AI capability are rising fast. Apple's answer is hybrid edge-cloud — privacy is both the shield and the constraint.

## What to Watch

- **Foldable iPhone**: beta code is there, announcement timeline unclear
