**Deciphering Google DeepMind CEO's Thought Process**

## TL;DR
Gain insight into Google DeepMind CEO's thought process through his passion for tackling "hard questions" and explore the underlying philosophy and strategy.

## What is it
Demis Hassabis, CEO of Google DeepMind, is a scientist and entrepreneur with in-depth research in artificial intelligence and deep learning. In a speech, he mentioned his fondness for "hard questions." Simply put, these are problems that appear difficult, complex, and challenging.

## Why is it important
Hassabis's passion for "hard questions" reflects his spirit of scientific and technological inquiry. He believes that only by solving seemingly insurmountable problems can human knowledge and technology truly advance. This attitude is also evident in DeepMind's research and development, such as the AlphaGo project, a quintessential "hard question."

## How it works
Hassabis's thought process can be described using the following flowchart:
```mermaid
graph LR
    A[Propose problem] -->|Identify challenge|> B[Analyze complexity]
    B -->|Find key|> C[Design solution]
    C -->|Experiment and verify|> D[Evaluate and improve]
    D -->|Iterate|> A
```
This flowchart reflects Hassabis's approach to tackling "hard questions": first, propose a problem and identify the challenge; then, analyze complexity and find the key; next, design a solution and experiment to verify; finally, evaluate and improve, repeating the iteration.

## Difference from general problem-solving approaches
Unlike general problem-solving methods, Hassabis's approach emphasizes addressing "hard questions" and challenges. General problem-solving methods might avoid seemingly difficult problems, whereas Hassabis actively seeks out and solves them.

## Conclusion
Hassabis's thought process is suitable for those with a strong spirit of scientific and technological inquiry. Researchers and developers in fields like artificial intelligence and deep learning can find inspiration and insight in his philosophy and strategy.
