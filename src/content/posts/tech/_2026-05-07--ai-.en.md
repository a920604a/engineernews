Why Is Your AI Agent Getting Dumber? How to Solve This Problem?

## TL;DR
Poor performance of AI agents may be due to inadequate training data and supervision.

## What Is It
An AI agent is an artificial intelligence system designed to mimic human behavior and decision-making. It is typically trained using machine learning algorithms to learn and improve its performance.

## Why Is It Important
The performance of AI agents directly affects the effectiveness of their application scenarios, such as customer service robots and smart assistants. However, many AI agents perform poorly in actual applications, even becoming less intelligent over time.

## How It Works
The workflow of an AI agent is as follows:

```mermaid
graph LR
    A[Data Collection] --> B[Data Preprocessing]
    B --> C[Model Training]
    C --> D[Model Deployment]
    D --> E[User Interaction]
    E --> F[Data Collection]
```

In this workflow, data collection and preprocessing are critical steps. Without adequate training data and supervision, AI agents can easily fall into the traps of overfitting or underfitting, leading to poor performance.

## Difference from Other Machine Learning Models
Compared to other machine learning models, the unique feature of AI agents is their ability to mimic human behavior and decision-making. However, this also means that AI agents require more training data and supervision to ensure their performance meets expectations.

## Conclusion
Poor performance of AI agents may be due to inadequate training data and supervision. To solve this problem, it is essential to focus on the quality of data collection and preprocessing, while providing sufficient supervision and feedback mechanisms. Suitable application scenarios for AI agents include customer service and smart assistants that require mimicking human behavior and decision-making.
